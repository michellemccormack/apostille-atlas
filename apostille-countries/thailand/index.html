Apostille for Thailand

Thailand has joined the 1961 Hague Apostille Convention, but the HCCH status table gives February 28, 2027 as the date it takes effect for Thailand.[source] Until then, plan on the authentication and legalization route for U.S. documents.

Sending a U.S. document to Thailand

Until February 28, 2027, an apostille is not the finished product for Thailand. The usual route for a non-Hague destination is a state authentication certificate, sometimes a further U.S. Department of State certificate, and then legalization by Thailand’s embassy or consulate. How apostilles differ from authentication certificates.

If your document will be presented on or after February 28, 2027, ask the receiving office in Thailand whether it will accept an apostille instead.

Using a document from Thailand in the U.S.

A public document issued in Thailand gets its apostille from Thailand’s own competent authority. This is what Thailand has registered with the HCCH:

Department of Consular Affairs of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs[source]
